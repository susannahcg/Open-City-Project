Open City Project

What does it mean to move and make a home in the city?

About

The Open City Project was a three-year ESRC funded project (2020-2023) looking at the social and political life of the city to test whether the utopian ideal of the Open City exists in real life. It explored issues of race, migration, mobility and living with diversity, and how the city accommodates new forms of urban life, through the social configuration of its spaces and places. The project looked at the ways urban governments reflect, promote or limit the idea of the Open City, focusing at the city-wide, borough and street/housing block scale.
